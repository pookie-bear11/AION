Make progress feel like an adventure.

AION wraps your study, habits, and personal goals in a lightweight RPG loop. You pick a quest, do the real-world task, then record it to earn XP, build streaks, and unlock cosmetic badges and collectibles.

It is an independent project, not affiliated with the AION game, and it runs entirely in your browser. Your progress is a motivation tool; AION cannot verify that a task was completed, and XP and collectibles are fictional rather than real-world prizes.

  • Completing a quest awards that quest's listed XP, once.

  • The Daily Drop gives 50 XP once per day.

Frequently asked questions.

Is this an official AION game service?

No. AION here is an independent, fantasy-inspired productivity RPG and is not affiliated with the AION game.

Do I need an account or password?

No. There is no real account system. You enter a display name to open the experience on this browser. Please do not enter real credentials or sensitive information.

Will my progress sync to another device?

No. Progress is saved in this browser's local storage. It does not sync between devices, and clearing browser data (or using Reset in Settings) removes it.

How do quests and XP work?

Each quest lists the XP it awards. Marking a quest complete grants that XP one time. Every 1,000 XP raises your level.

Can I create my own quests?

Yes. You can create, edit, and delete custom quests, or use the generator to preview template-based suggestions and add the ones you like.

Does the quest generator use AI?

No. It picks from a small set of built-in templates based on the category, difficulty, and time you choose.

What is the Daily Drop?

A small XP bonus you can claim once per calendar day from your dashboard.

Are rewards real-world prizes? Can AION verify my tasks?

No to both. XP, badges, and collectibles are fictional and cosmetic, and AION cannot check whether you actually completed a task. It is a motivation tool.

Is the leaderboard made of real players?

No. The ranking compares you with illustrative demo rivals so you can see how your XP stacks up.
